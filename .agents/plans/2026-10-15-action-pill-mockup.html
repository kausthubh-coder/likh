Sizes and rules these frames follow
  • Shown at 1.5× over a bright page, the same backdrop brand.html uses. The pill is surface, with the brand's edge recipe and caption/small type only.
  • Listening: the 24px glyph circle is inset 6px on the left, then the 7 bars (46px), then 15px. That makes 100 × 36, the same width as hands-free. Hands-free while in action mode adds the stop button on the right: 124 × 36.
  • Running, result, notes and confirm hug their words × 36. Error and approval use the existing 52px error/problem layout.
  • Colors keep their jobs: peach for being heard and the one primary button, green only on a finished notify, red only on an error.
  • Nothing here animates while idle. The wave and the ring move only while their state is on screen.
  • Not built or rendered in iced yet. This mockup is HTML only.
