Why "delete that" is narrower than the audit said

It acts in two cases only. Said as the whole dictation, likh presses Ctrl Z in the app in front. Said as its own sentence after others, it drops the sentence before it. "Delete that file" inside a sentence is typed as words.

The real risk is the first case after you switch apps: Ctrl Z lands in the new app. likh already knows which window it pasted into, so it can skip the undo there and say "Nothing to undo here".
