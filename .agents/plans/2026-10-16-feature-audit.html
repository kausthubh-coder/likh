Talking is easy; everything else hides

Hold Ctrl Space, talk, let go is intuitive, and the pill names a fix for most failures.

Past that, a user meets 6 triggers, 8 tabs and 23 settings with almost no teaching, and the worst failures make no sound at all.

  1. Fix problems 1, 2 and 4 first? They are a copy line, a reused flag and a default.
  2. Want a plan for the merges below (8 tabs to 6)?

Problems 6 to 14, with fixes
  1. Three ways to say "do something". Commands (fixed phrases, talk key), Actions (your phrases, action key) and Clean up all act on what you say. Lists come three ways ("new bullet", Clean up, an action); corrections two ("scratch that", Clean up's "actually 3"). Fix: a line on each tab naming the other; merge later.
  2. "Take it back" hides what it does. A group's explanation shows only while it is off, and all three groups that ship on are on. The chips "scratch that", "undo that", "delete that" press Ctrl+Z in whatever app has focus, so dictating "Delete that." undoes something. Fix: always show the explanation; drop "delete that".
  3. Clicking the Groq card switches engines without a key. The next dictation fails with "Add your Groq key in likh's settings, under Transcription." Fix: switch only once a key is saved.
  4. One ChatGPT call, two switches in two tabs. Clean up and Fix misheard names each say they add about a second; together they still add one. Without Codex both do nothing and log nothing. Fix: one group in Transcription, greyed out without Codex.
  5. A failed transcription loses the recording. "Can't reach ChatGPT. Check your connection and try again." means say it all again, even after 20 minutes hands-free. Fix: keep the last failed audio; paste last retries it.
  6. A stray tap leaves the mic open. A press under 300 ms starts hands-free, and the quiet stop only counts after speech, so silence records until a tap, Esc or 30 minutes. Fix: end it after 10 s with no voice, as "No voice heard".
  7. Starters assume the developer's tools. "New T3 thread" opens t3code:// for every user, and chips show raw {style}. Fix: ship it as .example.toml.
  8. Diagnostics is a top-level tab of step names ("cleanup", "transcribe") meant for bug reports. Fix: a link from App.
  9. Mode names drift. "Toggle" means "Tap to start, tap again to paste", and "Stop after quiet: Hands-free only" stays live in Hold, where hands-free can't happen. Fix: rename Toggle to Tap; hide the row in Hold.
