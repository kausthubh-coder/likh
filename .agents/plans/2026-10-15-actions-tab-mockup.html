A seventh tab for your actions

Actions sits after Commands: the built-in phrases there, yours here. It lists each action as its files say, flags the broken and the changed, and makes the simple ones without code.

  2. Should each action have its own switch, saved in its file as enabled = false? I'd say yes.
  3. Should the action key live on this tab, not on Dictation? I'd say yes.
