# Dictation

The product: hold Ctrl+Space, talk, let go, and the text pastes where the cursor is. The pill at the bottom of the screen shows listening, thinking, a countdown in the last minute, and errors. `likh ctl` drives the same path without keys.

## Sub-features

- `dict-paste` pastes the transcript into the focused app and puts the old clipboard text back.
- `dict-tap` a press shorter than 300 ms is a tap: hands-free in "Hold or tap", dropped quietly in "Hold only". Measured from the moment the key came up, not from when the dictation thread got to it (opening the mic holds that thread for 300 to 600 ms).
- `dict-cancel` Esc while recording (or `likh ctl cancel`) throws the audio away. Esc never reaches the app while recording.
- `dict-errors` shows a sentence in the pill for each failure: expired or missing Codex sign-in, ChatGPT refusing it, offline, too long, rate-limited, a busy clipboard, no microphone.
- `dict-retry` a transcription that failed in a way that may pass (offline, a busy or broken server, the sign-in, a missing Groq key) keeps its audio: the pill's problem has a `Retry` button, and `likh ctl retry` or paste last (Alt+Shift+Z) send it again with the engine picked now. A dictation that pastes, or one too long to send, drops it; a stray tap or a silent recording leaves it. Never for "No voice heard", a muted mic or a too-short tap.
- `dict-shortcut-taken` another app holding the shortcut at launch: the pill says "Another app has Ctrl + Space, so likh can't hear it." with `Change keys` (opens Dictation already listening for keys), the Dictation tab says "Another app has these keys." under the caps, the tray's line says "Another app has Ctrl + Space. Open likh to change it.", and setup's shortcut step leads with "Another app has these keys". Paste last's row on History says the same for its keys.
- `dict-no-voice` a recording with no voice in it (under 120 ms by the voice detector) is never sent: the pill shows a grey "No voice heard" for 3 s, no sound. A mic that sent only digital silence (muted, or blocked by Windows' privacy settings) shows "No sound from <mic>. Is it muted?" with a Pick mic button (Windows' sound settings), or "Windows is blocking likh from the mic." with Open settings; the error sound plays. While held, 2 s of digital silence puts "<mic> is silent. Is it muted?" in the pill until sound arrives.
- `dict-paste-last` Alt+Shift+Z (History tab: Change, Turn off) pastes the last dictation again once its keys are up; `likh ctl paste-last` does the same. Nothing yet: a grey "Nothing to paste yet". After a restart it pastes the newest history entry (history on). The tray's "Copy “…”" line, or `likh ctl copy-last`, puts it on the clipboard instead. Ignored mid-dictation.
- `dict-mic` Dictation's Microphone row picks the input: "Default · <mic>" follows Windows, or one mic by name (saved by id). Test opens it and moves seven bars until Stop or another tab; the mic is never opened just because the window is. A picked mic that's unplugged stays listed "(not connected)", the row says what's used instead, and dictations record from Windows' default. Bluetooth picks get a call-quality line. Unplugged mid-dictation: it pastes what it heard with "<mic> was disconnected. Pasted what it heard." The pill's Pick mic opens this row.
- `dict-language` Transcription's Language row: Auto-detect (the default, nothing sent) or one of 62 languages by English name, sent as its ISO-639-1 code: a `language` part to ChatGPT, a `language` field to Groq. An unknown saved code is auto-detect. Checking that ChatGPT honours it needs a real transcription with the person's sign-in: ask first.
- `dict-cap` counts down the last minute in the pill and stops at 30:00.
- `dict-long` past 3 minutes the audio goes up in pieces while you talk, each cut at the quietest moment of its last 10 s; the pieces' text is joined in order at the end, with one cleanup pass over all of it. A piece with no voice is skipped, a dropped connection retried once, and a piece that still fails fails the whole dictation.
- `dict-cleanup` Transcription's Clean up (off by default): with a Codex sign-in, the same ChatGPT pass as the dictionary drops "um"s and false starts, keeps the speaker's correction ("2, actually 3" is "3"), and puts spoken lists on `- ` lines. It never answers a request it hears, and an answer that lost or added a spoken command ("new paragraph", "press enter") is thrown away for what was heard. About 1 to 1.5 s more.
- `dict-status` `likh ctl status` reports the phase, the transcriber (`codex`, `groq` or `echo`), the dictation count and the last one's audio length, latency and error. Never the text.
- `dict-no-voice-stop` hands-free that hears no voice for 10 s (a stray tap) ends as a grey "No voice heard" and throws the audio away, unless Stop after quiet is "Never". A held shortcut never does.
- `dict-silence-stop` in hands-free, once a voice was heard (a voice detector, so one quiet word counts and a fan or typing never does), likh stops and pastes after the chosen quiet (3, 5 or 10 s; 5 by default). After 1 s of quiet the pill shows a peach ring around the stop button that empties toward the stop, with the seconds in the last 3 s; talking again clears it. A held shortcut never stops by itself.
- `dict-commands` "new line", "new paragraph", "new bullet" anywhere; "press enter" / "press tab" at the end; "scratch that" alone undoes the last paste only in the window that paste landed in (anywhere else: a grey "Nothing to undo here", no Ctrl+Z), after a sentence it drops that sentence; punctuation by name ("comma", "question mark") only when switched on. Each group can be switched off.
- `dict-allowance` when ChatGPT says the plan's allowance ran out (429 `usage_limit_reached`, ChatGPT Free after its monthly cap), likh pastes what it heard, saves the reset in `$LIKH_HOME/allowance.json` and makes no model call until then (Clean up, names, the action router, `ask`). The pill says once "Clean up is off until 17 Nov: ChatGPT's allowance ran out."; Transcription's Clean up and Dictionary's Fix misheard names say "Paused until 17 Nov: your plan's allowance ran out."; `ctl status` has `allowance.paused_until`. Transcription itself never uses it.
- `dict-dictionary` words on the Dictionary tab come out spelled their way: with "Fix misheard names" on and a Codex sign-in, ChatGPT (`gpt-6-luna`) swaps a misheard word for the entry only where the sentence is about it ("to currently and korean" becomes "to Critly and Kriyan", "I'm currently learning Korean" stays). About 1.3 s more per dictation; a failure or a 4 s wait pastes what was heard. Groq also gets the words as its prompt.
- `dict-engine` the engine in settings is read at every key-down: Codex or Groq (key from the credential store).
- `dict-pause-media` with "Pause music and videos" on (the Windows default), what's playing pauses at key-down and plays again at key-up. Only what likh paused.
- `dict-mouse` a picked mouse button (middle, back, forward) works like the shortcut and its normal click is blocked.
- `pill-idle` the pill is hidden and nothing (pill, shortcut, dictation) uses CPU while idle.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Run `likh`. Hold Ctrl+Space in any app, talk, let go.

## Driving it with scripts

Preconditions:

- A release build (`cargo build -p likh --release`).
- A fake `CODEX_HOME` (`bun scripts/fake-codex-home.mjs connected`) and, for repeatable runs, a speech WAV: `powershell -File scripts/tts-wav.ps1 -Text "Remind me to send the deck tomorrow at nine." -Out .agents/qa/speech.wav`.
- likh started in a background shell with the fakes, and its PID captured by path:
  ```sh
  LIKH_AUDIO="$(pwd)/.agents/qa/speech.wav" LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:Remind me to send the deck tomorrow at nine." \
    CODEX_HOME="$(pwd)/.agents/qa/codex-connected" ./target/release/likh.exe run --skip-onboarding
  ```

Recipes:

- **End to end, quietly (the default).** Add `LIKH_PASTE="$(pwd)/.agents/qa/pasted.jsonl"` when starting likh, then `powershell -File scripts/e2e-dictate.ps1 -Likh target/release/likh.exe -Seconds 3.4 -Expected "Remind me to send the deck tomorrow at nine." -PasteFile .agents/qa/pasted.jsonl`. It prints JSON; `matches` must be true, and `pressed` lists keys likh would have pressed. Nothing on screen changes.
- **End to end, through the real clipboard.** The same without `LIKH_PASTE` and `-PasteFile`: it clicks into its own box first, so the person's mouse moves. Only when the clipboard path itself is what you're proving.
- **The pill on screen.** Start `powershell -File scripts/screenshot-pill.ps1 -Out .agents/qa/pill/run -Count 10 -DelayMs 1500` in the background, then run the end-to-end recipe. Open the frames: a 76 x 36 capsule with peach bars while listening, grey bars while thinking.
- **Silence stop and commands.** Make the audio: `scripts/tts-wav.ps1 -Text "Meet at two. Scratch that. Meet at three, press tab." -Out .agents/qa/speech.wav`, then append 8 s of silence (Python's `wave` module: copy the frames, then add zero bytes). Put `{ "mode": "tap", "silence_stop": "3s", "pause_media": false, "sounds": false }` in `$LIKH_HOME/settings.json`. Start likh with that WAV and `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:Meet at two. Scratch that. Meet at three, press tab."`, plus `LIKH_PASTE`, then `scripts/e2e-dictate.ps1 -Likh target/release/likh.exe -Seconds 15 -Expected "Meet at three" -LetSilenceStop -PasteFile <the log>`. Expect `matches: true`, `pressed: ["Tab"]` and `start_to_stop_ms` about 3 s past the speech (8.2 s for this sentence).
- **The dictionary.** Put `"dictionary": ["Critly", "Kriyan", "Studi"]` in `$LIKH_HOME/settings.json`, start likh with `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:I pushed the onboarding fix to currently and korean last night."` and the real `CODEX_HOME` (the fix sends this text, never audio, to ChatGPT with the person's sign-in), then `e2e-dictate.ps1 ... -Expected "I pushed the onboarding fix to Critly and Kriyan last night." -PasteFile <the log>`. Expect `matches: true` and `latency_ms` about 1500. `cargo test -p likh cleanup_live -- --ignored --nocapture` checks the fix alone, including a sentence it must leave alone, and the cleanup (fillers, a correction, a list, a request, commands).
- **Groq for real.** With a key the person gave you for it: `GROQ_API_KEY=... LIKH_TEST_WAV="$(pwd)/.agents/qa/speech.wav" LIKH_TEST_TEXT="Remind me to send the deck to Priya tomorrow at 9." cargo test -p likh groq_live -- --ignored --nocapture`. It checks the key, refuses a made-up one, and transcribes the WAV with likh's own request code (about 0.5 to 0.7 s for 3.6 s of speech, 2026-10-12). Write numbers as digits in the expected text: Whisper does.
- **No voice, muted mic.** Make a muted WAV (4 s of zero samples) and a room WAV (4 s of noise around -50 dBFS, no voice) with Python's `wave` module. Start likh with each as `LIKH_AUDIO`, plus `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER=echo:...` and `LIKH_PASTE`. `likh ctl start`, wait 3 s, `likh ctl stop`, then `likh ctl status`: nothing new in the paste log, and `last.error` is "No voice heard" (room) or "No sound from muted.wav. Is it muted?" (muted). `scripts/screenshot-pill.ps1` during the run shows the mic-silent note from 2 s, then the problem pill with its button.
- **Paste last.** With a fresh `LIKH_HOME` history and `LIKH_PASTE`: `likh ctl paste-last` adds nothing to the log; dictate once, then `ctl paste-last` and `ctl copy-last` add `{"paste": …}` and `{"copy": …}` with the same text; quit, start again, `ctl paste-last` pastes it from history. Injected keys aren't used for the shortcut itself: check likh owns it by trying `RegisterHotKey` for Alt+Shift+Z from PowerShell (it fails while likh runs, works after quit).
- **Clean up.** Put `{ "cleanup": true, "dictionary": ["Critly"], "pause_media": false, "sounds": false }` in `$LIKH_HOME/settings.json`, make `tts-wav.ps1 -Text "Um, so, uh, I think we should, um, ship it on Friday."`, start likh with that WAV, `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:<the same text>"`, `LIKH_PASTE` and the real `CODEX_HOME` (text only goes to ChatGPT), then `e2e-dictate.ps1 ... -Seconds 6.3 -Expected "I think we should ship it on Friday."`. 2026-10-15: matched, latency 1157 ms.
- **A long dictation in pieces.** Make a WAV over 3 minutes (310 s: `tts-wav.ps1` on a long paragraph, then the frames twice with Python's `wave`), start likh with it, `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:One piece."` and `LIKH_PASTE`, and run `e2e-dictate.ps1 ... -Seconds 312 -Expected "One piece. One piece."`: one piece went at 3:00, the rest at the stop, so the fake's text comes back twice. `ctl status` shows `audio_seconds` for the whole recording.
- **Retry.** Start likh with a speech WAV, `LIKH_PASTE` and `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="flaky:Send the deck by Friday."` (the fake's first transcription fails as offline, later ones return the text). `ctl start`, `ctl stop`: nothing pasted, `ctl status` shows "Can't reach ChatGPT…" as `last.error`, and the pill (Windows) shows it with `Retry`. Then `ctl retry` (or `ctl paste-last`): the log gets `{"paste": "Send the deck by Friday."}`. A second `ctl retry` adds nothing.
- **ChatGPT's allowance runs out.** Delete `$LIKH_HOME/allowance.json`, put `{ "cleanup": true }` in settings, and start likh with `LIKH_ALLOWANCE_OUT=$(( $(date +%s) + 26*86400 ))`, a speech WAV, `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER="echo:Um, so, send the launch notes by Friday."`, `LIKH_PASTE` and the fake `connected` `CODEX_HOME` (nothing is sent). Two dictations: both paste the text as heard; the trace has one `allowance` warning and one failed `cleanup` span, the second dictation none; `ctl status` shows `allowance.paused_until`; Transcription's Clean up row says "Paused until …".
- **No voice in hands-free.** A room WAV (noise, no voice) as `LIKH_AUDIO`, `{ "mode": "tap", "silence_stop": "5s" }`: `ctl start`, wait 11 s, `ctl status` shows `phase: idle` and nothing pasted.
- **Without a screen.** `likh ctl start`, wait, `likh ctl stop`, then `likh ctl status`: `dictations` goes up and `last.audio_seconds` matches the WAV.
- **Tap and cancel.** `ctl start` then `ctl stop` within 0.1 s, or `ctl start` then `ctl cancel`: `dictations` doesn't change.
- **Idle cost.** `powershell -File scripts/measure-idle.ps1 -ProcessId <pid> -Window 60`: expect about 2 MB private, about 11 MB working set and 0 ms CPU.
- **Stop it:** `likh ctl quit`.

## Gotchas

- The real shortcut can't be scripted: likh ignores injected keys on purpose (so its own Ctrl+V paste can't trigger it). Ask a person to press Ctrl+Space, or use `likh ctl`.
- On a locked screen, pastes fail with "The native clipboard is not accessible" and screen grabs fail. Check for a `LogonUI` process before blaming likh.
- Never leave likh running with `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER=echo:…` when you hand the machine back: the person's next Ctrl+Space would paste your test sentence. `likh ctl quit` first.
- A real transcription (no `LIKH_TRANSCRIBER`) sends audio to ChatGPT with the person's own Codex sign-in. Ask before doing that.
- Pause media pauses the person's own music. Keep `"pause_media": false` in your `LIKH_HOME` settings unless that's what you're proving, and ask first. `cargo test -p likh media -- --ignored --nocapture` only lists the sessions.
- Never pick a mouse button in a running likh unless asked: the hook blocks that click system-wide. Injected clicks are ignored by design, so it can't be scripted anyway.
